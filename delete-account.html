Delete your Noori account

Noori accounts are anonymous: we never ask for your name, email or phone number, and we never keep your voice recordings.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Noori and go to Me (Profile).
  2. Tap Delete My Account (children: a parent answers a short verification question first).
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted at once.

What is deleted

Everything linked to your account: lesson progress, XP, streaks, quests, league history, recitation scores, subscription entitlements, and your display name. Nothing is kept afterwards. Downloaded Quran text and audio on your phone are not personal data and stay until you uninstall the app.

If you no longer have the app

Because accounts are anonymous, we cannot tell which account was yours without the app. Accounts that are not used for 12 months are deleted automatically, with all their data.
