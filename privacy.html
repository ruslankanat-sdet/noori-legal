Noori Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

Noori ("we", "our", or "the app") helps learners of all ages read, listen to, and recite the Holy Quran. We designed Noori around strict data minimization and child privacy.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

  • No personal contact details: We never ask for your email address, phone number, real name, physical address, or location.
  • No voice recordings stored: When you practice recitation, your audio is transmitted over HTTPS to our pronunciation scoring service, analyzed in memory to return word-by-word Tajweed feedback, and discarded immediately. Voice recordings are never saved to disk or used to train models.
  • No third-party ads or tracking SDKs: Noori contains no advertising SDKs or cross-app trackers.

2. Information Stored for App Functionality

  • Anonymous Account ID: On first launch, Noori creates a random anonymous identifier so your learning progress is saved.
  • Age Group & Child Mode: On first launch, Noori asks your age to map your profile to child (under 13), teen (13–17), or adult (18+). Only the group label is stored, never your exact age or date of birth.
  • Learning Progress & Scores: Lesson crowns, XP, daily streaks, timezone (for midnight quest/streak resets), and per-ayah recitation scores are stored linked to your anonymous ID.
  • Subscription Entitlements: If you purchase Noori Premium via Google Play or RuStore, we store the store purchase token and subscription expiration timestamp solely to verify and unlock your premium access across sessions.

3. Children's Privacy

Users in Child Mode (under 13 or age not set) cannot set or display a custom name; they always appear as "Learner" if leagues are enabled, and can opt out of weekly leagues completely. Sensitive actions—changing settings, opening external links, purchasing subscriptions, or deleting the account—are protected by a parent verification gate.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

  • Delete anytime in the app: Go to Me → Delete My Account to permanently erase your anonymous account and all associated progress, scores, and entitlements immediately. See Delete your Noori account for instructions.
  • Automatic cleanup: Anonymous accounts unused for 12 months are automatically and permanently deleted.

5. Third-Party Services & Attribution

  • Quran Content: Quran text, translations, and word-by-word audio are provided by Quran.Foundation and the Tanzil Project (tanzil.net).
  • Payments: Subscriptions are processed directly by Google Play Billing or RuStore Pay. We never receive your credit card or payment details.
